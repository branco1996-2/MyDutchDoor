For US citizens

Americans buy homes here too. The paperwork is just different.

Because of FATCA, each Dutch lender sets its own rules for US clients. Some ask for a US TIN or a CLN, some lend if you hold a payment account with them, and some non-bank lenders aren't subject to FATCA at all.
