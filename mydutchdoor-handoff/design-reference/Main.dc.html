For US citizens

Americans buy homes here too. The paperwork is just different.

Because of FATCA, the US law that makes foreign banks report on American account holders, each Dutch lender sets its own rules for US clients. Some ask for a US TIN or a CLN, some lend if you hold a payment account with them, and some non-bank lenders aren't subject to FATCA obligations at all.
